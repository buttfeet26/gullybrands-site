Questions.

Where can I buy Y2K crop tops in India?

Right here. Gully Brands makes Y2K graphic crop tops for it girls: coquette bows, leopard, 8-balls, slogans and grunge prints. Every crop top is ₹499 with free shipping anywhere in India.

What size crop top should I buy?

Our crop tops run from XS (32" chest) to XXL (42" chest), with a cropped length of 15.5" to 20.5". If you're between sizes, size up for a relaxed fit. The full chart is in our size guide.

What fabric are the crop tops made of?

Super-combed, pre-shrunk 100% cotton at 180 GSM: soft, breathable and made to keep its shape. Wash inside out in cold water and iron inside out to keep the print looking fresh.

How long does delivery take?

Every piece is printed fresh for you. Orders are printed, packed and dispatched within 2–3 working days, then arrive 3–5 working days later, anywhere in India (about a week in total). You get a tracking link by email as soon as it ships.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery?

No. We take secure online payments only: UPI, cards, net banking and wallets via Razorpay. Shipping is always free.

Can I return or exchange my crop top?

Because every crop top is printed on demand just for you, we don't take returns for change of mind or wrong size. If it arrives damaged, misprinted or wrong, email us within 7 days of delivery and we'll replace or refund it, no drama.
