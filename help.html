Shipping & Delivery

Every Gully Brands piece is printed on demand — made fresh for you when you order.

  • Production: your order is printed, packed and dispatched within 2–3 working days of confirmation.
  • Delivery: 3–5 working days after dispatch, anywhere in India — about 5–8 working days from order to doorstep (27,000+ pin codes served).
  • Charges: FREE shipping on every order, all over India. No hidden fees at checkout.
  • Payment: Secure online payments only — UPI, cards, net banking and wallets via Razorpay. We do not offer Cash on Delivery.
  • Tracking: you'll get an email + tracking link (AWB) as soon as your order ships.

If your order hasn't arrived within 10 days of the shipping email, write to us with your order ID and we'll chase it down.

Returns & Exchange

Because every product is printed on demand just for you, we don't accept returns for change of mind or wrong size selection — please check the size guide below before ordering.

We WILL replace or refund, no drama, if:

  • the product arrives damaged or defective,
  • the print is wrong or badly misprinted,
  • you received the wrong item or size (our mistake).

To claim: email us within 7 days of delivery with your order ID and clear photos of the issue. Approved claims get a free replacement, or a full refund to your original payment method within 7–10 working days.
